Featured Projects

1. Vantara FMCG Sales Analytics

Repository: vantara-sales-analytics on GitHub 🔗
Introduction

An end-to-end sales analytics project simulating commercial performance for a fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG) enterprise operating across Nigerian regional hubs.

Problem Statement

Management lacked visibility into profit margin variations across distribution channels, resulting in high logistics overheads and unoptimized inventory allocation.

Data Sourcing

Primary transactional dataset containing sales orders, product SKUs, distributor tiers, shipping logistics, and regional revenue records.

Columns

Order_ID, Order_Date, Region, Product_Category, Sales_Channel, Revenue, Unit_Cost, Units_Sold, Margin

Data Transformation & Cleaning

Cleaned raw dataset using Power Query in Excel: removed duplicates, adjusted datetime formats, replaced missing regional codes, and established calculated margin columns.

Before Cleaning

Unstructured raw sales entries with missing values and mismatched data types.

After Cleaning

Normalized, validated, and structured schema ready for data modeling.

Data Modelling

Created a Star Schema connecting Fact Sales tables to Dimension tables for Regions, Product Categories, and Date Hierarchies.

Analytics & Measures

Calculated total sales revenue, average profit per order, regional performance ratios, and YoY channel growth rates.

Dashboard & Visuals

Built an interactive Excel Dashboard featuring custom PivotTables, dynamic KPI summary cards, and regional slicers.

Insights & Findings

Direct sales channels generated 35% higher net margins compared to third-party distributors despite lower overall volume.

Recommendations

Reallocate marketing budget toward direct-to-consumer digital channels to improve overall product profitability.

Conclusions

Data-driven inventory re-routing across regional hubs can reduce annual holding costs significantly.
