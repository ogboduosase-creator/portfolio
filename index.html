About Me

Data Analyst. Co-founder.

I am a Data Analyst with hands-on experience across banking, private sector operations, and active remote analytics work. I collect, clean, transform, and analyse structured datasets — delivering dashboards, reports, and financial insights that teams and organisations can act on with confidence.

At Sterling Bank, I served as Settlement & Reconciliation Officer — cleaning and reconciling high-volume financial transaction data, automating reporting workflows in Excel, and designing dashboards that reduced discrepancies and improved reporting speed. Before that, I spent nine years analysing sales, customer, and inventory data at Elixir Packaging Enterprise, building dashboards and automated reports that cut manual processing by 40%.

I currently work as a freelance Sales Analyst, using Excel and SQL to track sales performance, clean and structure sales data, and produce reports that help businesses understand what's working and where the gaps are. Alongside this, I am co-founder of SabiBiz — a business clarity and growth platform helping Nigerian entrepreneurs stop guessing and start tracking.
